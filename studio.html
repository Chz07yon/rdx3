Studios

Where RDX RED's work actually happens.

Kokkada Main

Where RDX RED began, and where the majority of full-scale production still happens. Kokkada Main is built for control — a space shaped around light rather than around furniture, where every session starts from a blank, adjustable canvas instead of working around a room. This is the studio for productions that need room to build: full lighting setups, extended shoots, and the kind of unhurried creative process that a founding space allows.

Location: Kokkada, Dakshina Kannada, Karnataka, India

Status: Currently Closed

Mysuru Suite

A tighter, more precise space built for work that lives or dies on detail — portraits, brand campaigns, and the kind of editorial output that needs a controlled environment without the scale of a full production studio. Where Kokkada Main is built for range, Mysuru Suite is built for focus: fewer variables, faster turnaround, and a setup tuned for close, deliberate work.

Location: Mysore City, Karnataka, India

Status: Open Online
