## ABOUT
===================================
Founded: 2024, in Karnataka, by Chris Zeyon Pinto

Origin story:
RED Studio was founded in 2024 in Karnataka by Chris Zeyon Pinto with a simple but powerful belief — every story deserves to be told through light, motion, and emotion. What started as a photography and videography studio quickly evolved into something bigger. Today, RED is a full-spectrum creative house that captures moments through the lens and builds unforgettable digital experiences through code.

About RED:
RED Studio is more than a creative agency. We are storytellers, light painters, and digital architects. We specialize in cinematic photography, high-end videography, short-form content, and now premium website development under our CODE RED division, and impactful digital media design through our SHAPE RED wing. Every project — whether a single frame or an entire digital ecosystem — is crafted with obsessive attention to emotion, contrast, and detail. Our work doesn't just look good. It feels alive.

Vision:
We believe the future belongs to creators who can master both light and code. Our vision is to become Karnataka's most respected creative powerhouse — a studio where photographers, cinematographers, and developers work side by side to deliver experiences that are visually stunning and technically flawless.

Closing line: We don't just create content. We build legacies.
