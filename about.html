Our Architecture of Creativity

Ignited in 2024 within the vibrant core of Karnataka, RDX Studios emerged from a singular conviction: that exceptional storytelling demands mastery over both the visual and the virtual. What originated as a relentless pursuit of cinematic lighting and photographic perfection quickly expanded into a formidable three-pillar creative ecosystem.

Today, RDX RED captures the soul of the moment through luxury cinematography, Code RED engineers robust digital experiences for the modern web, and Shape RED forges strategic brand identities. Together, they form an uncompromising studio committed to building digital legacies.
